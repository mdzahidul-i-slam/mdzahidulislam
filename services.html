Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How long does a typical project take?

Most single-page projects take 3–5 days. Full websites or brand identity packages typically take 7–14 days depending on scope and revision rounds.

How many revisions are included?

Every package includes at least two revision rounds. Additional rounds can be added if the scope changes significantly from the original brief.

Do you offer ongoing support after delivery?

Yes. Websites include a support window after launch, and SEO or marketing work is available on an ongoing monthly basis.

What information do you need to get started?

A short brief covering the goal, target audience, examples you like, and any existing brand materials is enough to begin the discovery call.
